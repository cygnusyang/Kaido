The missing ledger

AI can write code.
Projects still need a plan.

When work lives in chat history, it is hard to see whether dependencies are complete, dates conflict or tasks are done. KaidoPlan makes the plan readable, writable and reviewable.

01 / DECOMPOSE

Turn requirements into a WBS

Organize work as tasks, groups and milestones. AI agents can import, read and update plans through MCP or the CLI.

02 / SCHEDULE

Inspect dates, predecessors and the critical path in the macOS Gantt workbench, and surface plan violations.

03 / TRACK
